# Aurora Solar Pump Co. — Site Permit and Compliance Guide

> Fictional sample document for RAG POC testing. All values are invented.
> This guide plays the role of the "permit document" in the POC corpus:
> dense rules, exact distances and deadlines — the kind of text where a
> RAG system must retrieve the exact passage, not a similar one.

## Setbacks and placement

- A pump installation must be at least **4.5 metres** from any property line.
- A pump installation must be at least **15 metres** from a septic system.
- A pump installation must be at least **30 metres** from a drinking-water well
  owned by a neighbouring property.
- Solar panel ground mounts must be at least **3 metres** from a public footpath.

## Permits

- Installations with a panel array larger than **10 square metres** require a
  county site permit, form **AUR-PERMIT-7**.
- The permit application must be filed at least **21 days** before installation.
- The permit fee is **$260** for residential and **$740** for agricultural sites.
- An approved permit is valid for **180 days** from the issue date.
- Installations inside a flood zone additionally require form **AUR-FLOOD-2**
  and a raised equipment pad of at least **45 centimetres**.

## Inspections

- A pre-installation inspection is required for all agricultural sites.
- The final inspection must be booked within **10 working days** of completing
  the installation, and the inspector requires the permit number, which starts
  with the prefix **AURP-**.

## Noise

- The installed system must not exceed **45 decibels at the property line**
  between 22:00 and 06:00.
