# Aurora Solar Pump Co. — Billing and Plans FAQ

> Fictional sample document for RAG POC testing. All values are invented.

## Plans

**Q: What does the Pro 3000 cost on the annual plan?**
The Pro 3000 costs **$1,899 per year** on the annual plan, billed once a year.

**Q: What does the Pro 3000 cost monthly?**
The monthly plan is **$179 per month**. The annual plan saves $249 over a year.

**Q: Is there a plan for the Lite 800?**
Yes. The Lite 800 costs **$499 per year** on the annual plan or **$49 per month**.

## Payments and refunds

**Q: What is the refund window?**
Full refund within **45 days** of purchase, no questions asked. After 45 days,
refunds are pro-rated and a **$75 restocking fee** applies.

**Q: Which payment methods are accepted?**
Visa, Mastercard, Amex, and ACH bank transfer. We do **not** accept PayPal.

**Q: When is a late payment charged?**
Invoices unpaid after **14 days** incur a late fee of **1.5% per month**.

## Warranty

**Q: How long is the warranty?**
The pump carries a **5-year warranty**. The controller carries a **2-year
warranty**. The solar panels carry a **10-year performance warranty**.

**Q: Does dry running void the warranty?**
Yes. Running the pump dry for more than **60 seconds** voids the pump warranty.
