AURORA SOLAR PUMP CO. - SUPPORT RUNBOOK (FICTIONAL SAMPLE)
===========================================================

ESCALATION
----------
Level 1 support response target: 4 business hours.
Level 2 (field technician dispatch) response target: 24 hours.
If a customer reports a burning smell from the controller, escalate
immediately to Level 3 and tell the customer to disconnect the PV array.
The Level 3 emergency phone line is staffed 24x7.

COMMON CASES
------------
Case: Customer says flow dropped after a storm.
Action: Ask them to clean the intake filter first; storms wash debris
into the intake. If flow is still low, book a Level 2 visit.

Case: Customer asks for the mechanical seal kit.
Action: The part number is ASP-SEAL-24. It fits all Pro models.
It does NOT fit Lite models; Lite uses ASP-SEAL-L1.

Case: Customer's permit inspection failed on setback distance.
Action: Do not advise moving the pump yourself. Refer the customer to
the compliance team and quote the 4.5 metre property-line rule from
the Site Permit and Compliance Guide.

RETURNS
-------
Returns are handled by the billing team, not support.
Support may quote the 45-day full-refund window from the Billing FAQ
but must not promise a refund amount.
